Our Story

SkillBridge Nepal was born from a simple observation: too many talented Nepalese graduates struggle to translate their academic achievements into meaningful careers.

Founded in Kathmandu, our journey began with a small team of educators, technologists, and industry leaders who saw an opportunity to leverage technology for social impact. We started by organizing local mentorship meetups, quickly realizing that scaling these connections required a dedicated digital platform.

Today, SkillBridge Nepal serves thousands of users across the country, facilitating skill exchanges, career recommendations, and mentorship relationships that were previously inaccessible to many. Our community continues to grow, driven by the belief that knowledge shared is knowledge multiplied.

Every success story on our platform reinforces our commitment to building a more skilled, connected, and opportunity-rich Nepal for future generations.
